Changes made for this Neural Designer example:
- The dataset copy has been adapted for use with Neural Designer.
- Adaptations may include delimiter conversion, selection of fields or records,
  renamed fields or labels, type conversion, and model-specific preprocessing.
- Only the complete E2 run-to-failure experiment was used; its source archive SHA-256 is 209A6D7692C893B3FC326765D9052CBDB70CBCCA8244CD40EACEAA32215F4086.
- Each five-second, 25.6 kHz acquisition was divided into five non-overlapping one-second windows.
- The distributed columns are time-domain and relative spectral-power features extracted from each window.
- The anomaly label is an Artelnics-derived teaching label: windows from the final 10 percent of acquisitions are 1 and all earlier windows are 0; it was not supplied by the original authors.
- The complete reproducible transformation is tools/prepare-bearing-replacement.py in the repository.
