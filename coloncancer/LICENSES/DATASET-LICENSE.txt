Changes made for this Neural Designer example:
- The dataset copy has been adapted for use with Neural Designer.
- Adaptations may include delimiter conversion, selection of fields or records,
  renamed fields or labels, type conversion, and model-specific preprocessing.
- Only etype=1 recurrence records were retained, giving one row per patient; etype=2 death records were not used.
- The final recurrence_within_5_years column is the binary classification target: yes means recurrence was observed within 1,825 days; no means the patient was recurrence-free through that horizon.
- The 41 patients censored before 1,825 days were excluded because their five-year outcome is unknown, leaving 888 usable patients.
- Patient ID, the constant study and etype fields, and follow-up time were omitted after deriving the fixed-horizon target; time would otherwise leak outcome information.
- Treatment, sex, clinical binary flags, differentiation, and local extent codes were decoded to descriptive categories.
- The 18 missing lymph-node counts were filled with the recurrence-cohort median; missing differentiation values were assigned the explicit unknown category.
- The redundant node4 field was omitted because the canonical documentation reports an unresolved inconsistency between nodes and node4.
- The distributed data files and hashes below identify this adapted copy.
