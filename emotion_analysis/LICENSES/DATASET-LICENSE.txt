records for anger, fear, joy, love, sadness and surprise from the original
43,410-row training split. Multi-label records and other categories were
omitted, numeric labels were mapped to names, identifiers were removed, and
TSV quoting was applied. Neural Designer stores the unchanged OpenNN output
under its legacy project path emotion_analysis.txt.
